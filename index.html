The Reality: Why Traditional Approaches Fall Short

🔄 Vendor-Environment Gap

There's always a gap between your vendor's security solutions and your actual environment. Traditional tools can't see the full picture of relationships and dependencies.

🤖 AI-Accelerated Threats

Attackers are operating at a higher level now, accelerated with AI. They think in graphs, exploiting relationships across identities, devices, and workloads.

📊 Siloed Data Views

Traditional tabular approaches force defenders to think in silos. Security teams query lists instead of understanding the interconnected web of your digital estate.

The Shift Needed

Defenders need to move from reactive, siloed approaches to proactive, interconnected defense. They need to see what attackers see: the graph of relationships that enables lateral movement, privilege escalation, and data exfiltration.

How Attackers Think in Graphs

An attack chain doesn't follow a simple linear path. It exploits relationships across your entire digital estate. Click the buttons below to see different attack scenarios.

Phishing Attack Scenario

The attack starts with a phishing email. The user downloads a malicious document, compromising their device. The attacker then pivots to the Key Vault, compromises an admin account, and gains access to AWS S3 buckets and cloud workloads. Traditional tools might catch individual alerts, but only a graph reveals the full attack path and blast radius.

Graph-Based Defense Advantages

  • 🎯 Attack Path Analysis: Prioritize weaknesses by considering the attacker's perspective
  • 🔍 Attack Surface Discovery: Continuously discover and contextualize your organization's assets
  • 💎 Critical Asset Identification: Identify the assets you should care most about
  • 💥 Blast Radius Understanding: Understand potential downstream impact of attacks

Microsoft Sentinel Data Lake & Graph Architecture

Microsoft Sentinel provides a unified, tenant-wide data lake with graph analytics capabilities that power experiences across Microsoft's security ecosystem.

AI Services Layer & Model Context Protocol (MCP)
Enables AI agents to access security context through natural language. Security Copilot and custom agents can query and reason over your security data without complex data engineering.
Interactive Tier: Graph | Tabular | Vector
Graph: Models relationships for threat investigations and attack path analysis
Tabular: Traditional structured data queries and analytics
Vector: AI-powered semantic search and embeddings
Data Lake Tier
Asset Store: Users, devices, network, systems, code, and data inventory
Activity Store: Logs, events, and actions across your environment
Content Store: Threat intelligence, policies, and configurations
Integration Services (Sentinel Connectors)
Ingests data from assets across on-premises, multi-cloud (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure), SaaS applications, endpoints (Windows, Linux, macOS, Android), IoT, and more.

Four Layers of Graph Intelligence

Security Layer

Asset configuration and topology - the foundation of your digital estate

Attack Layer

Theoretical paths possible - what attackers could exploit

Threat Intel Layer

Known attacker TTP paths based on threat intelligence

Incident Layer

Actual paths observed during real attacks and incidents

Graph-Powered Defense in Action

01

Attack Path Discovery

Proactively identify and visualize potential attack paths before attackers exploit them. See how a compromised user account could reach your critical assets.

02

Blast Radius Analysis

Instantly understand the full impact of a compromised asset. "What could happen if this admin account is compromised?" - answered in seconds, not hours.

03

Threat Hunting

Hunt for threat actor activity across the attack graph. Traverse relationships to find indicators of compromise that span multiple security domains.

04

Incident Investigation

Investigate attacks on the graph with queries. Quickly pivot from alerts to full graph-based context for deeper analysis and faster resolution.

05

Predictive Shielding

NEW: Automatically identify attack paths and apply targeted just-in-time hardening actions to critical assets ahead of where attacks might go.

06

Attack Disruption

Contain attacks by taking action to stop threat actor progress. Automatically block connections and isolate compromised assets based on graph analysis.

Real-World Attack Disruption Example

A real customer scenario showing how graph-powered defense stopped a domain compromise:

T+0: Attacker gains initial access using compromised credentials
T+4 min: Network scanning and payload execution detected
T+32 min: Domain Admin compromise - Disruption triggered
